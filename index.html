1 unicometre (unim) = 0.5 metres = 1,000 pegametres (pgm). 1 diometre (dim) = 0.005 m = 0.5 cm. A hand is 0.1016 m; a yard is 0.9144 m; a standard fathom is 1.8288 m. The needle shows the value divided by the multiplier in the small dial window, on a 0–10 scale. Negative altitudes are shown with a minus sign digitally and the needle indicates their magnitude. The gauge is not a certified aviation dial. GPS altitude may be unavailable or inaccurate, especially indoors. The browser's altitude is typically referenced to the WGS84 ellipsoid, not necessarily sea level. No location data is sent by this app to a server. Keep the app open while measuring.
